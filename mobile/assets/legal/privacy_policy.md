# Privacy Policy (Template)

_Last updated: 2026-02-22_

## 1. Who we are
UAPay is a demonstration fintech-style wallet application built for educational purposes.

**Contact:** replace-with-your-email@example.com

## 2. Data we collect
- Account data (email, user id)
- Profile data you add (name)
- Wallet and transaction data (balances, transfers, buys/sells)
- Technical logs (basic server logs, security events)

## 3. Why we collect data
- Provide authentication and access control
- Execute transfers and record transaction history
- Prevent abuse (rate limiting, fraud prevention)
- Maintain and improve the service

## 4. Where data is stored
Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) and processed by the backend server for payment events.

## 5. Payments
Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card details on our servers.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request:
- Access, correction, deletion
- Export of your data

## 7. Security
We apply row-level security in the database, secure headers and rate limiting on the backend.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date.
